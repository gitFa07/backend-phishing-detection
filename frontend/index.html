What it can't tell you

It reads the address, not the page

The model never opens the site. Page content, redirects and security certificates aren't part of the score.

It doesn't know the domain's reputation

Two sites with similarly shaped URLs score alike, whether the domain is new or years old. No blocklists or DNS lookups are used.

A score is not a verdict

Words like login or verify raise the score, but plenty of real pages use them. Treat the percentage as a reason to look closer.
